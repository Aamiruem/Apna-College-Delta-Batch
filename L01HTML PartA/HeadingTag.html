Understanding HTML Headings

Why structure matters — headings, accessibility, and SEO.

What are headings?

Headings provide semantic structure to a document.

Heading levels

h1 — Page title

Usually one per page.

h2 — Major sections

Use for major section headings.

Accessibility tips

Keep headings concise; use skip-links and logical order.

Conclusion

Semantic headings are essential for all users and search engines.
